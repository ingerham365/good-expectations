Featured perspective

The AI Advantage Is Becoming Operational

The first wave of business AI was about access: who had the newest model, the cleverest prompt, or the most ambitious demo. That advantage is disappearing as capable tools become available to everyone.

The durable edge now comes from operations. Teams that connect AI to clear decisions, trusted data, repeatable workflows, and human judgment will create value that a standalone tool cannot.

Start with one expensive bottleneck. Define what better looks like, build the smallest useful workflow, and measure whether it improves speed, quality, or customer experience. The companies that learn this discipline now will not simply use AI—they will build better businesses around it.

Jordan IngerhamFounder’s note · 4 min read

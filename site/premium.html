How it works

Where this data comes from — and why timing works the way it does.

Step 01

Sourced from filings

Every trade in this feed is drawn from public congressional financial disclosures, compiled from Capitol Trades’ filing database.

Step 02

Disclosures can lag

Under the STOCK Act, filings can arrive up to 45 days after the trade happens. That delay is built into the law — not the feed.

Step 03

Always refreshing

This feed reloads automatically every 15 minutes, so each trade appears the moment its disclosure goes public.
